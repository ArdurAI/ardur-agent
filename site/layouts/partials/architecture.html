Architecture at a glance

You reach the agent through a channel; the gateway hands off to the runtime; the runtime coordinates providers, memory, tools, and skills. Cap-tokens, receipts, and cost-tuples thread through every edge.

A turn runs ten stages in order — cap-token, Cedar policy, cost-gate, hooks, injection scan, provider, receipt, hooks, cost finalize, memory, journal — short-circuiting on the first failure. Cap-tokens guard what tools can run; cost-tuples bound what providers can spend; receipts record all of it.
