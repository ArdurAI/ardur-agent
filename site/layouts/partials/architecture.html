Architecture at a glance

You reach the agent through a channel; the gateway hands off to the runtime; the runtime coordinates providers, memory, tools, and skills. Cap-tokens, receipts, and cost-tuples thread through every edge. 

flowchart TD
  U["You

  G --> R["Runtime

  R --> P["Providers

  R --> T["Tools
OS-isolated execution"]
  R --> S["Skills & Plugins
MCP · ACP"]
  R --> H["Hooks
policy checks"]

  R -.emits.-> CT["Cap-tokens
scoped · revocable"]
  R -.emits.-> RC["Receipt-chain
signed · replayable"]
  R -.emits.-> CO["Cost-tuples
tokens · $ · time · attention"]

  CT -.guards.-> T
  CO -.bounds.-> P

Cap-tokens guard what tools can run. Cost-tuples bound what providers can spend. Receipts record all of it.
