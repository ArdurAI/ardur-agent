Why open source

Your personal AI should be auditable end to end. You should be able to read the code, run it on your own machine, and change what you don't like.

Closed-source personal AI is a bet on someone else's incentives staying aligned with yours — indefinitely. We'd rather not ask you to make that bet. The receipt chain proves what the agent did; the source proves what it can do.

Apache-2.0 licensed. Read it, fork it, self-host it, ship it.
